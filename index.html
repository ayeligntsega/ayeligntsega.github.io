01 / FILM & HUMAN EXPERIENCE
01 — THE FILMMAKER

I am an Ethiopian filmmaker interested in the quiet tensions of ordinary life — the people, places, memories and choices that shape who we become.

My path to cinema began in theatre and grew through a life connected to rural Ethiopia. I studied Theatre Arts at Addis Ababa University and have worked across acting, writing, directing and production. I am drawn to stories that observe people with patience, dignity and emotional honesty.

A CINEMATIC BELIEF

“Ethiopian cinema should draw from Ethiopian agriculture.”

For me, the lives of farmers, the land, labour, culture and memory are rich sources of cinematic identity — not limits on where our stories can go.

03 — BACKGROUND

From theatre
toward cinema.

Ayelign Tsega studied at Addis Ababa University’s School of Theatre Arts, graduating with distinction. His creative experience spans theatre, radio drama, television drama and feature filmmaking. Alongside directing and screenwriting, he has worked as an actor, assistant director and educator.

His work is informed by an enduring interest in everyday people and the worlds they inhabit.
